🪐 Genesis

A whole planet — oceans, forests, grassland, desert and ice, laid out by latitude & climate. Life is seeded from a single ancestral strand and left to evolve. Every creature's DNA literally is its code: a strand of A/C/G/T read as codons — the first few build the body, the rest are instructions for a little machine that drives behaviour. Mutations insert, delete, duplicate and flip bases, so the genome itself grows and changes. Tap a creature to read its DNA and the exact same thing as runnable code.
