🌐 Gaia

The self-replicating code of Soup, now planted on a world: a grid planet with resource biomes (rich greens, barren reds). Organisms harvest local energy to fund their CPU, divide into neighbouring land, and compete for territory. Three forces of open-ended evolution are switched on: predation / parasitism (steal a neighbour's energy), sexual recombination (cross genomes on birth), and an escalating environment — as life masters today's logic tasks, the world demands harder ones, so the bar keeps rising. Nobody wrote a fitness function. Tap any pixel of land to read the organism living there.
