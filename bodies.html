🧬 Morphos

Here a creature is not a dot — its DNA is a developmental program that grows a body out of living cells: structure, fins, mouths, sensors, photosynthetic and armour cells. What a creature can do emerges from its anatomy — fins make it fast, photo-cells let it feed on light (a plant), mouths + fins make a hunter. Mutations add, remove and duplicate body parts, so morphology — and its complexity — evolves. Tap anything to see the body its DNA builds.
