How to play — left thumb moves and jumps, right thumb aims and fires. Walk over a dropped gun to take it. Grunts keep coming; you get three lives. Turn your phone sideways for a much wider view.
For the best photo
  • Stand back so your whole body is in frame.
  • Arms slightly out from your sides.
  • Plain wall behind you, decent light.
  • Portrait orientation works best.
🔒 Nothing is uploaded. Your photo never leaves this device — the character is built entirely in your browser, and works offline.
