🌍 Biosphere

A living world built from the ground up: elements (light, water, carbon, oxygen, minerals) flow through a planet of mountains, plains, oceans, ice and forest, driven by seasons, rain, drought and snow. Life is assembled from those building blocks — plants photosynthesise CO₂ + water + minerals into biomass and O₂; animals eat biomass, breathe, and return nutrients when they die. Everything carries DNA and evolves. Matter cycles; nothing is free.
