A self-contained ecosystem. 🧬 Evolution
Every creature has a tiny neural-network brain whose weights are its genome. Brains mutate at birth, so behaviour is not scripted — fleeing, grazing, ambushing, swarming all have to evolve from whatever survives. Innate instinct only sets how much a brand-new lineage leans toward food / away from danger at birth; at 0 they start as blank slates (purest emergence, but they may struggle to bootstrap). Applies on Reset and to fresh creatures from Bloom — existing lineages keep evolving on their own. Crank Mutation strength (per species) to speed adaptation. Immigration lets rare new arrivals wander in, keeping the world alive and the gene pool fresh; set it to 0 for a sealed world that can go extinct for good.
